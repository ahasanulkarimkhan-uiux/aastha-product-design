The export record dates this set to 3 November 2026: fresh Chrome V5 capture, 1400 px wide, quality-95 RGB JPEGs, with HTML-only navigation excluded. This package does not use older V2–V4 previews or the separate image-generated Product Design cover. The later native editable Figma cover is also a separate artifact and is not represented as this local JPEG.

Device frames are CSS-authored presentation artwork inspired by a squared Android handset silhouette. They are not official Samsung photography or hardware specifications. Embedded screens are local HTML renderings from existing project design definitions, not exact Figma screenshot exports. Longer screens intentionally show selected initial content.

Names, medicine examples and times in the selected screens are demonstration content, not patient records. The selected panels include no participant photographs or interview transcripts. Research dialogue elsewhere in the case study is composite; no participant quotations or raw records are included in this repository.

Provenance: `behance-research/jpeg-export-manifest.json`, `jpeg-export-review.md`, `showcase-manifest.json` and the V5 brief/log. Original internal paths and audit files remain local.
